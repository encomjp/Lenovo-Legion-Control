# Changelog - 0.2.21 (2026-10-14)

Major GUI revamp. Every page was redesigned to be calmer and easier to read: one button style, plain wording, and no scrolling at the default window size. This release also adds automatic power-mode switching, a custom fan curve, a one-hour temperature history, and several fixes found through fleet telemetry. Tested on a Legion Pro 7 16AFR10H (83RU).

## New

- **Redesigned app**: every page was rebuilt around what you need most often.
  - The Control center shows power mode, temperatures, fans, battery and four quick toggles. Charge limit lives on the Power page, GPU mode on Performance.
  - One button design everywhere, with plain words instead of technical terms.
  - Advanced controls sit behind "Advanced", so the default view stays short.
  - The window opens at a size that fits your screen, remembers its size, and keeps content centred on wide monitors.
- **Automatic power mode**: choose a mode for "Plugged in" and one for "On battery" in Settings. Optional app rules switch modes while an app runs, for example Steam to Performance. A manual change holds until you plug in or unplug.
- **Mode popup**: a small on-screen popup shows the new power mode when it changes, including Fn+Q. It can be turned off in Settings.
- **Custom fan curve**: draw your own temperature to fan speed curve per power mode on the Cooling page. Above 90 °C every fan runs at full speed, and any sensor error hands control back to the firmware.
- **Temperature history**: the background service keeps the last hour, even while the app is closed. Mode changes are marked on the chart.
- **Undo**: applying a fan curve, power limits or Curve Optimizer shows "Undo" for 10 seconds.
- **Lighting saves itself**: effect, colour and brightness apply as you change them. Brightness is shown in percent with a live preview.
- **Health recommends what is missing**: optional components like the speaker firmware or the AMD tuning driver show up on Health only when your laptop needs them. The welcome screen is now a single page.
- **Idle fans** read "Idle" instead of "0 rpm".

## Faster

- The app starts about 35% faster by building each page when you first open it.
- While the window is hidden or in the background, the app stops or slows its updates, saving battery.

## Fixes

- **NVIDIA GPU temperature**: the background service can read the dedicated GPU again. Its sandbox blocked the device, which also made the fan curve fall back to the firmware.
- **Fans staying off**: the service no longer re-sends "automatic" to fans that are already automatic. On Gen 10 firmware this could keep fans stopped for longer.
- **AMD tuning driver**: "Install" in Settings now works on source installs.
- **Upgrades**: the installer now restarts the background service, so the app no longer reports a version mismatch after updating. It also removes old copies in `~/.local/bin` that could shadow the new version.
