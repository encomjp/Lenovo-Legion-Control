# Changelog - 0.2.22 (2026-10-14)

Safer fan curves and power limits on Gen 10 (2025) Legion laptops. Tested on a Legion Pro 7 16AFR10H (83RU), including suspend and resume.

## New

- **Fan curve survives sleep**: after waking up, the background service re-applies your fan curve, your Custom power limits and the charge limit.
- **Fan curve checks itself**: every fan speed it sets is read back. If the firmware does not accept the curve, the fans go back to automatic and the Cooling page says so.
- **Needs charger**: on battery the firmware controls the fans, so the curve pauses and the Cooling page shows "Needs charger". It resumes by itself when you plug in.
- **Power limits stay put**: Custom power limits are saved and re-applied whenever you switch to Custom mode, because the firmware resets them in every other mode. Each value is read back after writing.
- **New command**: `legion-cli power-limit` lists the limits, and `legion-cli power-limit <name> <watts>` sets one.

## Fixes

- **Max power only where it works**: every list of power modes (app, tray, KDE widget, command line) now comes from what the firmware reports. Models with a broken Max power entry, such as some Legion Go handhelds, no longer offer it.
- **Clear message for power limits**: changing a limit outside Custom mode now says "Switch to Custom mode first" instead of appearing to work while the firmware ignores it.
